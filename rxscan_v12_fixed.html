❓ Help Center
✕
📋 Parchi/Prescription kaise scan karein?
Home screen pe "Parchi Scan" tap karein → Camera ya Gallery select karein → Prescription ki clear photo lo → AI automatically parhega.
💊 Reminder kaise add karein?
Reminders screen mein jao → "+ Add New" tap karein → Medicine name, time aur meal timing fill karein → Save karein.
🍽️ Food calories kaise check karein?
Food Scanner open karein → "Scan Now" tap karein → Khane ki photo lo → AI calories aur nutrition show karega.
🌐 Language kaise change karein?
Settings → Appearance → Language tap karein → English, Roman Urdu, ya Urdu mein se chunein.
📧 Support se rabta karein
Koi masla ho toh: support@rxscan.pk par email karein. Hum 24 ghante mein jawab denge!
